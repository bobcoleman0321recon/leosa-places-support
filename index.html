LEOSA Places

A training and awareness aid on state carry law. This version covers New Jersey's sensitive place law, for retired officers, RPO holders and active officers carrying off duty.

Contact

For problems, wrong place names or outdated information, email leosaplaces@gmail.com. Please do not send details of a legal matter. The author cannot give legal advice.

Common questions

Why does my location not work? Open iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, Location Services, LEOSA Places, and choose While Using the App.

Why is there no street map? It is optional and needs a connection. Turn it on under Layers on the Map tab. The rest of the app works offline.

Why does a place say No known restriction mapped? That is not a clearance. Many restricted places are not on the map, and posted signs, owners and court orders control.
