Privacy Policy

LEOSA Places. Effective October 31, 2026.

LEOSA Places does not collect your data. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Your location

The app uses your location only when you tap Check my location or turn on Follow. The check runs on your device. Your location is kept in memory while the app is open. It is not saved, logged or sent to anyone.

What stays on your device

The app saves three settings on your device so they are there next time: the carrier type you picked, your street map choice, and a lawyer's phone number if you enter one. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. The Am I covered? self-check is not saved.

Optional street map

The street map is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your device loads map images from OpenStreetMap. Its servers can then see your internet address and which area you view, as with any website. The OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy covers that data. With the street map off, the app makes no network requests for map data.

Links

Citations and links open in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

What the app does not have

No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or data sales.

Children

The app is meant for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
